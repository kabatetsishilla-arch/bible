God’s Creation

God’s creation refers to everything that God made. According to the Bible, God is the creator of the universe and everything in it.

Main things about God’s creation
God created the world
God created the heavens, the earth, and everything in them.
Creation was done in six days
According to Genesis 1, God created different parts of creation over six days and rested on the seventh day.
Light and darkness
God created light and separated it from darkness.
Sky, land and seas
God separated the waters and created dry land, seas, and the sky.
Plants and trees
God created plants, grass, flowers, and trees to grow on the earth.
Sun, moon and stars
God created the sun, moon, and stars to give light and to mark days, seasons, and years.
Animals
God created fish, birds, wild animals, and other living creatures.
Human beings
God created man and woman in His own image (Genesis 1:27). Human beings were given responsibility to care for and manage creation.
God saw that creation was good
The Bible repeatedly says that God saw what He had created and that it was good.
The seventh day
On the seventh day, God rested from His work of creation and blessed the day.
